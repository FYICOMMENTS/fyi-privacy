Privacy policy

For FYI French and FYI Spanish · Last updated 12 October 2026

This policy explains how Ka Ying Yau, using the app brand FYI, handles information when you use these GCSE vocabulary apps. They are intended for learners aged 13 and older and are offered in the United Kingdom.

Information the apps store

Depending on the features you use, an app stores information in its private local database on your device. This can include a chosen name or nickname, course and tier, learned-word selections, practice answers and history, settings, Daily Stars and other progress, goals, exam attempts and results, and information entered about sponsors or rewards. Sponsor records may include names, a learner-written goal pitch, promised money or gifts, and delivery confirmations. Please enter only what is needed and avoid sensitive information.

The apps do not require an online account. They do not send learning profiles or study records to an FYI server, and they do not use FYI advertising or analytics. We do not receive those records through a cloud-sync service.

Why we use the information

We use locally stored profile and study information to provide the learning, progress, goal, exam and app-lock features you choose. Our lawful basis for this use is our legitimate interests in providing those features. We have considered necessity, learners’ reasonable expectations, and the rights and interests of learners aged 13 to 17. We use the information for these app features, not for advertising, profiling or analytics.

Speech practice

If you choose Spell & Speak, the app requests microphone and speech-recognition access. The app requires Apple’s on-device speech recognition; audio is not stored or sent by the app. A final transcript you submit may be saved in the local practice history. You can use typing when speech recognition is unavailable or you do not grant access.

Tips and Apple services

Optional tips are one-off purchases through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple handles payment; the app does not receive or store payment-card details. The app receives the product and purchase result from Apple and stores limited local status to handle pending or completed purchase messages. Apple processes purchase and device-service information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Face ID, Touch ID and device-passcode checks are provided by iOS. The app receives an authentication result, not biometric data. Apple device backup may include the app’s local database if backup is enabled in your device settings. Apple and your settings control backup storage and restoration.

Where information is stored and who receives it

Learning and profile information stays in the app’s private local database on your device. The apps have no FYI user account, cloud-sync service, advertising network or analytics service. We do not sell personal information or receive study records through a server. Apple provides operating-system functions, speech recognition, device backup and purchase processing as described above.

How long information is kept and how to delete it

App information remains in local storage until you erase it or remove the app. To erase a language app’s records, open About → Settings → Erase all data and confirm. This clears that app’s local profile, progress, settings, goals, sponsor records and exam records; it does not erase the other language app. Removing the app also removes its local data, but a copy may remain in an Apple device backup according to your settings. We cannot remotely erase a local copy or backup because the apps do not send those records to us.

Apple retains and manages information it processes for purchases and device services under its own policies.

Children and family use

The apps are intended for learners aged 13 and older and are not intended for children under 13. Learners can use a nickname and should avoid putting sensitive information in a name, goal pitch or sponsor field. A parent or guardian can help manage device permissions, purchases and backups through Apple’s device and family settings. The apps do not make decisions about learners with legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.

Your privacy rights

UK data-protection law gives people rights in relation to their personal information, including rights to request access, correction, erasure or restriction and, in some circumstances, to object to use based on legitimate interests. Most learning information is stored only on your device and is not available to us. You can view or correct information in the app and erase it using the steps above. For a privacy question or rights request, email fyi.comments@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Contact and this website

For privacy questions about FYI French or FYI Spanish, contact the publisher, Ka Ying Yau, at fyi.comments@gmail.com. We use messages sent to this address to respond to your enquiry or request. Gmail provides the email service.

This policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about visits to this page under its Privacy Statement. The apps themselves do not use GitHub Pages to transmit learning records.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. Please check this page for the latest version.
