FYI support

Help for FYI French GCSE Vocabulary and FYI Spanish GCSE Vocabulary.

Contact us

Email fyi.comments@gmail.com. Tell us which app you use, your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Do not send passwords, bank details or private pupil or sponsor information. Remove personal information from screenshots.

Pronunciation and speaking practice

Pronunciation uses voices available on your iPhone. Speaking practice requires microphone and speech-recognition permission and a supported on-device language model. If speaking is unavailable, use the typing option.

Your progress

Your profile and learning records are stored on your device. French and Spanish keep separate records. Deleting an app can remove its local records; your device backup settings determine whether a backup is available.

Erase your data

Open About → Settings → Erase all data and follow both confirmations. This resets the selected app's records.

Optional Koka tips

Tips are optional and processed by Apple. They do not unlock learning content or provide learning advantages. For billing or refund help, visit Apple's purchase support.
